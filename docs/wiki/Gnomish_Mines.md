# Gnomish Mines - NetHack Wiki

## Overview

The **Gnomish Mines** functions as a dungeon branch designed for adventurers seeking experience and tools. It spans eight or nine levels total, featuring special locations like Minetown and Mines' End.

## Generation

The Mines entrance appears on dungeon levels 2-4. Most levels function as simple caverns populated with gnomes, dwarves, gnome lords, and occasionally dangerous creatures like mind flayers (termed "minesflayers" when appearing out-of-depth).

Standard level features include:
- 6 random traps
- 1 tool
- 3 gem piles
- 3 random objects
- 50% chance of darkness

A mines level is often substantially harder than a level of the main dungeon at the same Dlvl, since its monster selection can ignore normal difficulty capping.

## Strategy Overview

### Race Considerations

Gnome and dwarf characters receive preferential treatment from native inhabitants. Lawful humans may be ignored by dwarves, though gnomes remain hostile to neutral humans.

### General Tips

**Elbereth Usage**: The technique involves "engraving with fingers for speed or blades for durability." This protective measure deters hand-to-hand combat from mine creatures.

**Stair Control**: Remaining near stairs allows quick escapes and crowd management.

**Pet Management**: Pets serve as combat assets and curse-testing tools. A leash proves valuable if found.

**Trap Avoidance**: "Watch where other creatures walk" to identify safe passages. Searching uncertain squares prevents accidents.

**Gray Stone Caution**: Kicking gray stones before pickup prevents acquiring dangerous loadstones.

### Four Stages of Exploration

**First Level**: Initial reconnaissance focusing on equipment acquisition rather than complete clearance. Target items include helmets, boots, daggers, and potentially mithril-coats or pick-axes.

**Upper Mines**: Methodical level clearing while accumulating loot and watching stat changes. Darkness significantly increases difficulty.

**Minetown**: Features unique mechanics including watchmen who may kill unprepared pets. The town provides temples, shops, fountains, and secure storage opportunities.

**Lower Mines**: Substantially more dangerous territory featuring polymorph and trap-door traps. Magic and poison resistance become essential at this stage.

Recommended entry point: around character level 5-6, not at level 1, unless there is a specific reason to go earlier.

## Variants

### SLASH'EM

Gnome and dwarf characters encounter transformed undead versions of normally peaceful inhabitants. Gnomes become zombies, gnome lords become mummies, and similar substitutions apply to dwarves. Pick-axes become rarer, though dwarf thieves in Minetown maintain probability of generation.

### FIQHack

Upper mine levels always remain lit while lower levels remain perpetually dark.

## Encyclopedia Entry

The mines represent dwarf craftsmanship, potentially deserted or minimally inhabited. Complex gallery systems feature carved engineering. Jewels remain embedded in walls, while metal appears primarily as finished armor and weapons.
