## The very basics

New players should master character movement, monster combat, and staircase navigation. The wiki advises: "take it slowly! Never use the auto-repeat feature of the keyboards: press each key deliberately and carefully."

Key movement commands include using diagonal keys, the underscore key for safe corridor travel, and angle brackets for stairs. The `S` key searches for hidden doors along walls.

When encountering monsters, consult the wiki first to assess combat viability. Maintaining HP above half its maximum is critical—retreat to heal before reaching dangerously low levels. After defeating enemies, eat their corpses to avoid starvation, but verify safety first.

Avoid melee combat with certain creatures like floating eyes, which can paralyze. Use ranged weapons instead. If HP drops below 6, the extended prayer command may help in emergencies, though overuse risks divine anger.

Writing "Elbereth" on the ground causes most monsters to flee and stop attacking.

## The early game

Early objectives involve completing Sokoban and the Gnomish Mines while acquiring essential supplies and resistances.

### Goals

- **Gain magic resistance and reflection**: These defenses counter powerful attacks. They require worn or carried equipment and are rarely randomly generated, making them prime candidates for early wishes.

- **Gain poison resistance**: Critical protection against poisoned attacks and traps. Several classes acquire this intrinsically, while others must consume appropriate monsters or use alternative methods like unicorn horns or tinning kits.

- **Complete Sokoban**: This branch contains wands, rings, and either a bag of holding or amulet of reflection—both valuable items. Its no-teleport property simplifies certain harvesting strategies.

- **Complete the Gnomish Mines**: Though dangerous due to coordinated dwarf and gnome attacks, Minetown offers shops, temples, and price identification opportunities. The bottom contains a useful luckstone.

- **Gather useful supplies**: Collect ascension kit pieces and other valuable items. Potions can be alchemized, scrolls blanked, and extra equipment saved for later polypiling.

## The mid game

This phase covers the remaining Dungeons of Doom, including the Quest and Castle. Players obtain a wand of wishing during this section to complete equipment gaps.

### Goals

- **Reach experience level 14**: Required for quest entry. This typically demands time or special items like potions of gain level or wraiths. Some players clear the Castle and Valley of the Dead before reaching this threshold.

- **Kill Medusa**: While digging past her island is possible, returning requires confronting her. Perseus's statue may contain levitation boots or shield of reflection. Reflection or blindness helps counter her stoning gaze.

- **Complete the Quest**: Different quests vary in difficulty. The quest artifact and Bell of Opening from the nemesis are essential for game completion.

- **Conquer the Castle**: Multiple methods work—levitation, tonal instruments, or striking wands to break the drawbridge plus jumping or diving. Musical instruments allow low-risk grinding. The Castle contains dangerous arch-liches; magic resistance is highly recommended. A wand of wishing awaits in corner towers.

## The late game

By this point, characters should possess most resistances, strong statistics, and excellent equipment. Gehennom generates wands twice as frequently as other dungeon areas.

### Goals

- **Navigate Gehennom**: This section consists mostly of mazes. Digging paths between stairs when possible reduces frustrating backtracking and mysterious force encounters.

- **Prepare for powerful demons**: Gehennom harbors formidable entities like Juiblex, Orcus, Baalzebub, and Asmodeus. Divine assistance becomes unavailable in these depths.

- **Kill Vlad the Impaler**: His tower entrance appears between dungeon levels 9 and 13 of Gehennom. The tower contains an amulet of life saving. Vlad carries the Candelabrum of Invocation, required for the invocation ritual.

- **Find the vibrating square**: Located at Gehennom's bottom on a level without downstairs, this cannot be detected through magic mapping and requires exploration. Finding it before angering the Wizard of Yendor is strategically wise.

## The end game

Careful preparation before confronting the Wizard of Yendor proves essential. Players should organize levels, pack necessary items, and mentally prepare for the final challenges.

### Goals

- **Kill the Wizard of Yendor**: His tower portal hides within one of two false towers. He curses inventory items, summons creatures or clones, and uses touch of death. He persists throughout the endgame and steals critical items. The Book of the Dead he carries is necessary for the invocation ritual.

- **Perform the invocation and retrieve the Amulet**: With all three invocation items, players access the Sanctum. Defeating the High Priest of Moloch yields the Amulet, but fake versions exist—verification is important.

- **Navigate the Elemental Planes**: The Wizard may reappear here. Levitation and portal detection prove valuable. Conflict reduces harassment from super-elementals.

- **Reach the Astral Plane and ascend**: Players must penetrate hostile priests, player monsters, angels, and the formidable Riders to reach their high altar. Sacrificing the Amulet of Yendor completes the ascension.
