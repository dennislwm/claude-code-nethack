# Elbereth (nethackwiki.com/wiki/Elbereth)

Fetched summary — for full detail see the live page.

## How to engrave

Press `E` (engrave command), then pick a tool. Fastest methods (one turn):
- Fingers (respond `-` when prompted)
- Wand (fire, lightning, or digging)
- Athame (non-cursed)
- Magic marker (4 charges)

The word must stand alone on the square with no extra characters.

## Durability

- **Temporary (dust/fingers)**: fast but fragile — ~4% fail chance per
  letter per attempt, degrades immediately from movement or combat.
- **Semi-permanent (athame/wand of digging)**: durable enough for routine
  combat, slower degradation, no charge cost for athames.
- **Permanent (wand of fire/lightning)**: won't degrade from standing or
  walking; costs wand charges — reserve for critical situations.
- Levitating after engraving keeps it from eroding, if already levitating.

## Monsters that ignore it

- Humans and elves (`@`)
- Angelic beings (`A`)
- Minotaurs, Riders, shopkeepers, guards, priests, the Wizard of Yendor
- Tame/peaceful monsters (won't attack anyway, but don't respect it either)
- Blinded *sighted* monsters (can't read it)
- Unique monsters (NetHack 3.7.0+)
- Eyeless creatures (e.g. gelatinous cubes) still respect it.

## What destroys it

- Attacking any monster while standing on it ("You feel like a hypocrite") —
  erases it and costs 5 alignment.
- Natural degradation, especially dust engravings.
- Engraving over it with another tool/wand.
- Movement over a temporary engraving.

## Does not protect against

- Ranged/spell attacks, missile fire, breath weapons (though a dragon
  prefers melee if adjacent and blocked).
- Damage from Gehennom or Endgame areas.
- Any square other than the one engraved — pets need their own.

## Practical use

Pre-engrave a durable Elbereth at hallway choke points/near stairs before
trouble starts. When surrounded: engrave, then wait for HP to recover.
Scuff it out before resuming attacks (standing on it while attacking erases
it anyway).
