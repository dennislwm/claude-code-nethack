# Valkyrie - NetHack Wiki

## Starting Inventory

Each Valkyrie begins with:

- A +1 spear (wielded), or dwarvish spear for dwarven characters
- A +0 dagger (alternate weapon)
- A +3 small shield
- 1-2 uncursed food rations
- An uncursed oil lamp (16.7% chance)

Non-pauper Valkyries gain knowledge of non-magical, non-polearm weapons and armor. The default pet is either a little dog or kitten.

## Intrinsics

Valkyries acquire special properties at specific experience levels:

- Level 1: Cold resistance
- Level 3: Stealth
- Level 7: Speed

## Attributes

Valkyries emphasize Strength and Constitution (30% distribution each), with Dexterity at 20%. Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma receive minimal allocation (6-7% each).

Human Valkyries start with 16 HP and 2 Pw; dwarven variants begin with 18 HP and 1 Pw.

## Skills

**Basic:** Saber, quarterstaff, trident, sling, attack spells, escape spells

**Skilled:** Pick-axe, short sword, broadsword, polearms, lance, two-weapon combat, riding

**Expert:** Dagger, axe, long sword, two-handed sword, hammer, spear, bare hands

Valkyries begin with Basic skill in spears and daggers, using Wisdom for spellcasting.

## Special Rules

- Never fumble while walking on ice
- Gain +2 bonus when casting emergency spells
- With 25 Strength, throwing Mjollnir returns with 99% success rate when wielded

## Rank Titles

Experience level progression displays as:

- XL 1-2: Stripling
- XL 3-5: Skirmisher
- XL 6-9: Fighter
- XL 10-13: (Wo)man-at-arms
- XL 14-17: Warrior
- XL 18-21: Swashbuckler
- XL 22-25: Hero(ine)
- XL 26-29: Champion
- XL 30: Lady/Lord

## Gods

Based on Norse mythology:

- Lawful: Tyr
- Neutral: Odin
- Chaotic: Loki

Their first sacrifice gift is Mjollnir.

## Quest

Valkyries seek "The Orb of Fate," an artifact crystal ball requiring only 8 Intelligence to apply successfully (versus 16-20 for others). The artifact provides warning, half spell damage, half physical damage, and acts as a luckstone. Its invocation enables level teleportation.

The quest involves defeating Lord Surtur amid fire traps, lava, and fire giants.

## Strategy Overview

Valkyries rank among the easiest roles in NetHack, emphasizing straightforward melee combat. Their starting spear serves effectively throughout early gameplay.

### Character Creation

Dwarven Valkyries appeal to both novices and speedrunners due to superior HP growth and infravision. Human lawful characters benefit from peaceful Gnomish Mines encounters. Neutral humans gain better late-game artifact options.

### Early Game

The +1 spear meets most melee needs; the +3 small shield provides excellent AC-to-weight ratio. Gnomish Mines offer abundant equipment. By dungeon's end, aim for AC roughly -5.

Mjollnir serves as a powerful first sacrifice gift, though lawful characters preferring Excalibur may pursue dipping strategies.

### Mid-Game

Achieve AC approximately -10. Consider learning scrolls of enchant weapon and enchant armor. The Valkyrie quest, while manageable, features fire traps and lava hazards requiring preparation. Lord Surtur proves vulnerable to cold-based attacks.

Two-weapon combat remains optional, trading shield protection for increased damage output.

### Late Game

The Castle presents manageable challenges for quest-completed Valkyries. Obtain magic resistance before Gehennom. Excalibur remains excellent when paired with silver weapons for two-weaponing.

The Orb of Fate reduces magic marker ink consumption through its damage-reduction properties and detection capabilities.

## History

The Valkyrie first appeared in NetHack 1.3d. Prior to version 3.6.7, they started with a long sword instead of a spear and gained stealth immediately rather than at level 3.

## Variants

**SLASH'EM:** Allows doppelganger race; starts with spear and dagger stack. Mjollnir uses heavy hammer base with +24 shock damage.

**SporkHack:** Permits any race; only Knights obtain Excalibur through dipping. Weight mechanics affect two-weapon offhand choices.

**dNetHack:** Adds yuki-onna (nymph) race option; starts with spear, bow, and arrows instead of daggers.

**NetHack Fourk:** Replaces Valkyrie role with Shieldmaiden; valkyrie becomes a playable race instead.

**SlashTHEM:** Extends race options to include giants, incantifiers, and nymphs alongside SLASH'EM variants.
